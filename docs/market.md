# Market research: quick scan

*As of 2026-11-27. A quick scan, not a study: about 90 web searches by four research agents, then twelve source pages opened to confirm the facts that matter. Each figure is marked **confirmed** (read on the provider's own page on 2026-11-26) or **search result** (seen only in a search-result summary; check before quoting). Estimates are labelled as such.*

**In short:** no Swiss provider was found that puts an operated server box into the customer's office for one monthly fee with public prices. The mainstream alternative for fiduciaries, law firms and practices is the opposite: a hosted desktop in the provider's Swiss data centre. Rechenbox prices are fair, not cheap: about what a conventionally replaced server with maintenance and backup costs, and a quarter to a half of the hosted-desktop list prices found, which include the Windows desktop. What competitors show and Rechenbox cannot yet show is a name, an address and customers.

## 2. What everyone says, and what nobody showed

**Table stakes.** Nearly every provider found says: data in Switzerland; data protection and professional secrecy; the CLOUD Act as the threat; a fixed monthly price; monitoring, updates and backup included; a personal local contact. These are expected and will not set Rechenbox apart.

**Not found at any competitor** (in search results and on the pages opened; a short search is not proof):

1. The combination: the server stands in the customer's office, the provider operates it, and hardware, backup device, firewall and UPS are in one monthly fee with the hardware refresh included.
2. Complete public prices including the setup fee and the term. Compu-Trade is the exception, for a service without hardware.
3. A restore test every month, with a written report. Compu-Trade includes one, two or four a year.
4. A portal in which office staff make everyday changes themselves, with every change logged.
5. What happens when hardware fails, spelled out per plan.
6. Exit terms: a full export and the option to take over the hardware at the agreed residual value.

What follows for the page:

- The fair comparison is with what the box replaces: server replacement, licences, NAS, firewall, maintenance contract and backup over the years.
- Never suggest the box is cheaper than Microsoft 365 or replaces it.
- A 15-person office on Business pays more per head than a hosted desktop that already includes the desktop. Business has to be argued with what it prevents (a day without IT), not with price.

Two claims on the earlier page did not hold up:

- **"Cyberversicherer fragen nach getesteten Backups"** could not be backed by a Swiss insurer's own wording. One insurer's conditions limit cover when no backup of at most a week old exists (search result).
- **"Berufsgeheimnis" for all readers.** As far as the research could tell without opening the law text, the statutory professional secret covers lawyers, notaries, doctors, dentists and auditors, but not fiduciaries as such, architects or engineers; their confidentiality rests on contract and on the data protection act. No rule of a professional body was found that forbids US cloud services: the guidance for lawyers and doctors sets conditions. Have a lawyer confirm before the page says more.

## 6. What comparable pages do

Seven landing pages were read (Swiss IT providers, a server-box maker, three business-software sites):

- The headline says what it is and for whom in five to seven plain words.
- One action, repeated, with one smaller step beside it.
- Proof follows the hero and is specific: a figure with its source, a standard by name, a person with a role.
- Money and terms are exact wherever they appear, including the minimum term and what is not included.
- None opens with a mission statement, and none puts technical specifications in the upper half.

Rechenbox has no customers to show before the pilot. What it can show is exactness: prices, terms, limits and the stage. What it still lacks, and competitors all have, is a legal entity with an address, a named person and a phone number.

## 7. Limits of this scan

- Quick by design: searches in German and English, no interviews, no calls to providers.
- Ordinary websites are blocked in the environment the research ran in, so only twelve named sites could be opened. Everything else is marked "search result".
- The own-server cost in section 3 is an estimate built from licence prices and assumed hours, not a quote.
- ServerBase and the META10 listing could not be opened even after they were allowed.
